# tinytalks.live — brand guide

## Concept

Two overlapping speech-bubble marks represent the two strangers in every
conversation; the negative-space dots between them read as a spark
(connection forming) or, at a glance, a friendly face — small, human, a
little playful. The name plays on "tiny" (quick, low-stakes, byte-sized
conversations) against "talks," and the gradient signals energy without
tipping into either "dating app pink" or "corporate chat blue."

## Logo files

- `logo-icon.svg` — square app icon / favicon / avatar placeholder mark.
- `logo-full.svg` — icon + wordmark lockup, for headers and marketing.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the icon's rounded
corner radius; don't compress the wordmark's tracking.

## Color palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink 950 | `#0b0e14` | App background (dark-first design) |
| Ink 900 | `#11151f` | Cards, panels |
| Ink 800 | `#171c2a` | Inputs, secondary surfaces |
| Coral 500 | `#ff6b5b` | Gradient start, warmth, primary CTAs |
| Violet 500 | `#8b5cf6` | Gradient mid, links, focus states |
| Mint 500 | `#2dd4bf` | Gradient end, success/positive states |

The brand gradient (`135deg, #ff6b5b → #8b5cf6 → #2dd4bf`) is reserved for
the logo and primary call-to-action buttons — using it everywhere dilutes
it. Everything else stays on the ink neutrals with a single accent color
per screen.

## Typography

- **Display / headings:** Sora (600–800 weight) — geometric, friendly, a
  little more distinctive than a default system sans.
- **Body / UI:** Inter (400–600 weight) — neutral, highly legible at small
  sizes for chat bubbles and dense admin tables.

## Voice

Warm, direct, a little wry — never cutesy, never corporate-legalese in
product copy (that's what the Terms & Conditions document is for). Safety
messaging (verification prompts, report confirmations) is always clear and
non-alarmist: explain what's happening and why in one short sentence.
