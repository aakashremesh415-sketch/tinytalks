## 1. Who can use tinytalks

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use tinytalks in
any capacity, including as a guest. By checking the age-confirmation box at
sign-up, you are making a legal representation that you are 18 or older.
Misrepresenting your age to access this service is a violation of these
Terms and may be reported to relevant authorities where we are legally
required or permitted to do so.

## 2. Account types

**Guest accounts** are temporary and require no email or password. Guest
accounts and all associated data (messages, media, session information)
are automatically and permanently deleted 2 days after creation.

**Regular accounts** require an email, a password, and a photo submitted
for gender verification, reviewed by our moderation team before your
claimed gender is marked verified. We use this photo only to confirm the
gender you've stated on your profile; it is not used for any other
purpose and is not shared externally.

## 3. Verification for image sharing

tinytalks does not collect government-issued identification from any
user, for any purpose. Before an account (guest or regular) can send or
receive images, it must independently complete two checks:

- **Email verification** — a one-time code sent to an email address you
  provide, confirming it's a real, reachable address.
- **Automated age estimation** — a live selfie processed by a third-party
  age-estimation service to produce an estimated age and confirm you are
  a real, live person (not a photo or recording). This service does not
  perform identity verification, does not check your image against any
  database, and is only used to estimate age.

Passing both checks is required before image sharing unlocks. This is
independent of, and in addition to, the gender-verification review
described in Section 2.

## 4. How self-destructing images work

Images you send can be set to a **10-second view** or a **one-time view**.
Once opened by the recipient, the image is removed from the chat display
according to that setting. Independently of what the recipient sees:

- Any image not manually deleted by its sender is automatically hidden
  from the chat interface 2 days after upload.
- The underlying file is permanently deleted 7 days after upload, unless
  the sender deletes it earlier (which removes it immediately).
- **During that window, our safety team may access images that are the
  subject of a user report, in order to investigate that report.** Every
  such access is logged internally. Images are not otherwise reviewed,
  used, or shared.

By using the image-sharing feature, you acknowledge and agree to this
retention and moderation process.

## 6. Reporting and enforcement

You can report another user directly from a conversation, selecting a
category that best describes the issue. Reports are reviewed by our
moderation team, who may warn, suspend, or permanently ban an account,
and — for the categories above involving suspected illegal activity —
refer the matter to law enforcement.

We keep a record of banned accounts (in hashed form, not your raw email
or personal data) specifically so that a banned user cannot simply create
a new guest or regular account to continue the same behavior after their
original account and its content are deleted or expire.

## 8. Privacy and data retention, in brief

- Guest account data is deleted after 2 days, in full.
- Gender-verification photos are retained only as long as needed for
  review and enforcement purposes.
- Age-estimation selfies are processed by our verification vendor and are
  not retained by tinytalks; we store only the resulting pass/fail
  outcome.
- Messages are stored on our servers and are not reviewed except when a
  conversation is the subject of a user report submitted by you or the
  other participant, for the purpose of investigating that report.
- Images follow the retention schedule in Section 4.
- Banned-user records are retained indefinitely in hashed form for abuse
  prevention.

A full Privacy Policy, covering data handling in more detail, should be
published alongside these Terms before launch.
